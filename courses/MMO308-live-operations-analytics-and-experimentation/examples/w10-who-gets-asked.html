The survey trigger decides who gets asked

An “online now” survey reaches each player in proportion to their playtime, so heavy players fill it. Here that flips a clear yes into a no. Weighting each answer by 1/π brings back the true answer, at the cost of a smaller effective sample.

Survey trigger (chance π a player is asked)
Model from the lecture. Segment k has Nk players, hk hours and sk logins per week, and approval yk. The trigger value tk is 1, sk or hk. Respondent share qk = Nktk / Σ Njtj, nk = n qk (unrounded, so the weighted estimate is exact). Weighted approval = Σ(nk/tk)yk / Σ(nk/tk). Effective n = (Σ nk/tk)² / Σ nk/tk². Everyone asked is assumed to answer; nonresponse is a separate bias. Segments and numbers are invented for teaching.
