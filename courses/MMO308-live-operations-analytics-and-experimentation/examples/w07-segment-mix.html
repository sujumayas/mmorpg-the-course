Every segment improved. The total fell.

A total rate is a weighted mean of its segments. A free weekend floods week 1 with free players, who retain worse. Both segments retain better than before, yet the dashboard shows a drop, because the weights changed. The waterfall splits the drop into a mix part and a rate part.

Formulas as in the lecture. M = wpaid·mpaid + wfree·mfree. Old-rate ordering: mix = Σ(w1 − w0)·m0, rate = Σ w1·(m1 − m0). New-rate ordering: mix = Σ(w1 − w0)·m1, rate = Σ w0·(m1 − m0). Either way mix + rate = ΔM exactly. This is Simpson's paradox in the form analysts meet it on dashboards. All numbers are illustrative.
