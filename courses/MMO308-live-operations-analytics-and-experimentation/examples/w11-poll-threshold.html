A supermajority line only decides the close calls

Old School RuneScape content needs a supermajority of Yes votes. Move the line from 75% to 70% and Sailing flips from fail to pass, along with a thin band of other questions. Most questions sit far above both lines. At low turnout, a very small group can block anything.
