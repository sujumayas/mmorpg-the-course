The leak you can't see, and the score that can

A dupe bug adds 1σ of gold a day from day 29. The top chart looks the same before and after. The CUSUM score in the bottom chart climbs out of the noise within days.

Hover or focus a chart, then use the arrow keys to read one day.

—first CUSUM alarm
—first spike alarm (z > 3)
—first 10% rule firing on or after leak start
—leak days until CUSUM alarm (start day counts as 1)
—gold leaked by then, L × σ × days
—10% rule firings before the leak began

σ = 1.0 million gold. The baseline is assumed known: each day's b is the true weekday value from BASE = [40, 39, 40, 41, 44, 52, 50], Monday to Sunday. The lab estimates it from past weeks. The 10% rule fires when x is above 1.10 × the mean of the previous 7 days, with BASE standing in for days before day 1. S does not reset after an alarm.
