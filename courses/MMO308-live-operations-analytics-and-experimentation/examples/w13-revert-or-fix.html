Revert first, even when you doubt it

A 60% hunch that the last patch broke the game is far more than you need. Reverting pays whenever your belief beats q*, and q* falls as the bug gets harder to find.

q is your belief that the last change caused the outage. p is the chance one diagnosis round of r minutes finds the cause. F is the time to ship a fix once found. R is the time to revert. N only scales player-hours.

Model: MMO308 week 13, section 2. Diagnosis rounds are independent, each finds the cause with chance p, so the round count is geometric (MA102 week 7). A revert either removes the cause (chance q) or returns you to the start of a fix-forward diagnosis. The numbers are invented for teaching. The simulation uses a seeded mulberry32 generator, seed 13.
