Model: each install day's players are a mix of stayers (daily survival 0.985) and tourists (0.30): R(c, a) = w × 0.985a + (1 − w) × 0.30a. An onboarding break lowers w for cohorts installed from day P. An endgame patch lowers stayer survival on calendar days after P. An outage multiplies every cell on calendar day O by m. Noise draws each cell as Binomial(installs, R) from a seeded generator. Invented game. The Destiny 2 case is discussed in the lecture.
