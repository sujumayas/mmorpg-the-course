Model from the lecture: batch increments Xi ~ Normal(θ/√K, 1), zk = Sk/√k, stop at the first |zk| > bk. θ = 1.96 + 0.8416 gives 80% power for one look at the end. Pocock and O'Brien-Fleming constants for two-sided α = 0.05 with equally spaced looks were computed for this page by recursive numerical integration (Armitage, McPherson and Rowe 1969) and checked with 1,000,000 simulated tests per K. Random numbers: mulberry32 with Box-Muller.
