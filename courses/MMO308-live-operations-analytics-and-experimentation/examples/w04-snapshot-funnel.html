Model: every day a player still playing with k steps done finishes the next step with probability q, quits with probability h, or stays put. A player who quits keeps the steps they reached. Reached step k at age t sums everyone, playing or gone, with at least k steps. "All installs" averages cohorts aged 1 to L days, as if installs were spread evenly since launch and the snapshot is taken on day L. Invented game.
