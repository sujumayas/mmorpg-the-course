# Data source and licence for osrs_polls.csv

`osrs_polls.csv` is derived from the poll pages of the Old School RuneScape Wiki (https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/), tallied for MMO308 in September 2026. It covers 3,304 yes/no questions from 14 March 2013 to 21 September 2026. The question text is trimmed to 160 characters.
