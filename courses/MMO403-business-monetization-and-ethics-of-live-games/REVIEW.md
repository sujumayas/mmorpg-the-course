## Fact sweep (29 September 2026)

Verified (7):
- South Korea in-force date (w07). law.go.kr shows Law No. 19242 of 21 March 2023 in force one year after promulgation, so 22 March 2024. Cited with the URL.
- Epic v. Apple certiorari (w12). Supreme Court docket 25-1311 shows the petition granted on 30 June 2026, limited to Question 1. Cited.
- Titan (w14). Polygon (23 September 2014) confirms the public cancellation. Kaplan's DICE 2017 talk (Gamasutra, archived) gives 140 developers, internal cancellation in May 2013, and about 40 left for the new concept. The w14 sentence that said the team passed 100 "years before" the stop was wrong and now uses the May 2013 date.
- Smash Land (w14). Gamasutra's GDC 2016 report ("4.6 people", 10 months) and Supercell's archived forum post (1 July 2015) replace the Wikipedia citation.
- Halo Infinite passes (w05). Polygon (14 June 2021) for non-expiring passes and IGN (17-18 November 2021, archived) for the XP change.
- Van Westendorp (w10). The four questions match Lipovetsky et al. (2011). The 1976 proceedings were not seen, and the source line says so.
- China 2019 minors' limits (w07). The 2021 NPPA notice on gov.cn cites the 2019 notice by number. The 200-400 yuan figures stay attributed to Xiao (2020).

Hedged (4):
- China 2016/2017 disclosure notice (w06). The notice was not found on gov.cn. The table row now says "as reported", and the source line names Gamasutra (8 December 2016) and Xiao et al. (2021).
- Austria Hermagor ruling (w07). The ruling is not in Austria's public database. The text says "reportedly" and treats the details as press reports.
- Camelot Unchained total of about $17M (w13). Still labelled as a reported figure from Wikipedia.
- PLEX 5,500 for 360 days and 450 PLEX for MCT (w11). The EVE support site blocked scripted fetches. The text attributes both figures to the EVE University wiki.
