The option to stop turns a losing bet into a winning one

Same game, same 25% odds. Funded all the way, it is worth −$5.00M. Funded in two stages with a vertical slice in between, it is worth +$3.70M, and the funder kills 65 of every 100 projects to get there.

—EV_all, $M
—EV_gate, $M
—value of the gate, $M
—kill rate after stage 1
—P(hit | go)
—break-even false-go rate f*

Stage-1 design

Model from the lecture. EV_all = pV − C1 − C2. EV_gate = −C1 + max(0, p·s·(V − C2) − (1 − p)·f·C2). Kill rate = 1 − (p·s + (1 − p)·f). P(hit | go) = p·s / (p·s + (1 − p)·f). f* = (p·s·(V − C2) − C1) / ((1 − p)·C2), clamped to [0, 1]. The pitch and its numbers are invented for teaching. Grid counts are rounded to whole projects.
