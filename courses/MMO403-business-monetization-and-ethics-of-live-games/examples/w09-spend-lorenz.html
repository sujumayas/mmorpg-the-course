Model: payers follow a Pareto distribution with shape α, so L_pay(F) = 1 − (1 − F)^(1 − 1/α) and G_pay = 1/(2α − 1). The all-player curve is the payer curve squeezed into the last p of players, so G_all = (1 − p) + p × G_pay. Cap cost: x_m = A(α − 1)/α, payers above C = (x_m/C)^α, revenue lost = (x_m/C)^(α − 1)/α, assuming capped players do not move the money elsewhere. Figures: Swrve, January 2014, via Recode (Johnson, 2014); Close et al. (2021), Addictive Behaviors 117, 106851; ARPPU $17.32 from King's Form F-1/A (Q4 2013). The toy server is invented.
