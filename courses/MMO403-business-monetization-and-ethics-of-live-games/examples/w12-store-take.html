Schedules as in the lecture. Steam: 30% to US$10M, 25% to US$50M, 20% above (Valve, from 1 October 2018). Epic Games Store: 0% on the first US$1M per product per year, 12% after (from June 2025; the threshold resets each 1 January). Google Play 15% tier, remaining markets: 15% on the first US$1M of annual earnings, 30% above (help page, September 2026). Apple Small Business Program: 15% up to US$1M for a developer under US$1M last year, 30% otherwise (page, September 2026). Direct store: a card processor at 2.9% plus US$0.30 per purchase (Stripe US standard pricing). Tax, refund and deal terms are invented for teaching.
