Model: straight-line demand c(p) = c0 − β(p − p0), clipped at 0. RPE = p × c. Break-even conversion c0 p0 / p1. Arc elasticity uses midpoints. Sample sizes per arm at a two-sided 5% test and 80% power: n_c = 7.849 (c0(1−c0) + c1(1−c1)) / (c1 − c0)², n_r = 7.849 (p0² c0(1−c0) + p1² c1(1−c1)) / (p1c1 − p0c0)², with 7.849 = (1.96 + 0.8416)². Simulated tests use a seeded generator: a normal approximation when n × c ≥ 10, exact binomial draws otherwise. All numbers are the lecture's invented teaching values.
