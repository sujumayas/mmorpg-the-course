Model from the lecture. Cash C is the cheapest set of whole packs whose coins, added to the balance b, cover the item; ties go to the purchase with more coins. Leftover L = b + coins bought − item price. Reference price = item coins × price of the smallest pack / its coins, rounded half up to the cent; markup = C / reference. Emberfall is an invented game; the flat grid has the shape of Guild Wars 2's gem store and the no-currency grid the shape of Final Fantasy XIV's online store, as described in the lecture.
