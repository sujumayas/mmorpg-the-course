A token above the subscription price can only lose money in the store

Each token month earns at least the token price minus the subscription it replaced. The token loses money only when its dollars come out of other store spending, or when a fee pushes the net token price below the subscription.

Model: ΔR = T × (c_net × (1 − σ) − k × s_net), with c_net = c(1 − f_c) and s_net = s(1 − f_s). Break-even σ* = 1 − k × s_net / c_net. The bars use net prices, so without fees they match N × s + k × T × s and N × s + T × c. Contours on the map mark ΔR = 0 and ±25% of the largest gain or loss on the map. T, N, k and σ are invented teaching values, not company figures. Prices: WoW US$15 subscription and US$20 token (Americas); Old School RuneScape US$14.99 membership and US$9.99 bond for 14 days, so US$21.41 per 30 days (September 2026, per the lecture).
