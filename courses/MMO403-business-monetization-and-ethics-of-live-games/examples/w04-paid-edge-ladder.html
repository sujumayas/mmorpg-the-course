Model from the lecture. A buyer with edge b beats an otherwise equal player with probability p = Φ(ln(1 + b) / (σ√2)). The rating gap that gives the same win chance is Δ = 400 × log10(p / (1 − p)), the Elo logistic scale. Ratings are normal with spread s; buyers sit Δ higher. The top-q cut-off t is found by bisection on [−3000, 3000], 100 iterations. Φ uses a series and continued-fraction erf accurate to about 1e-12. No real game's data is used.
