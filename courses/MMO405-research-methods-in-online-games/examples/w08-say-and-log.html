The survey gap is the log gap plus a bias gap

Group F logs 6.4 more hours a week than group M. The survey says 3.4, because F under-reports by 3 hours more. Push F's reporting bias a little further and the survey loses the difference, then reverses it. The logged bars never move.

—survey gap Δr
—log gap Δℓ
—bias gap bF − bM
—share shown Δr / Δℓ

Shifts every F answer; logs stay put
Lecture ten: invented players from the lecture's section 2. EverQuest II means: Williams, Consalvo, Caplan and Yee (2009), "Looking for Gender", survey answers linked to server logs (mid-2000s, four servers); here F is women and M is men. Identity: mean(r) − mean(ℓ) is the mean of r − ℓ, so Δr = Δℓ + (bF − bM) always holds. Interviewee rule: in each group, the two largest under-reporters (sort by d, then id) plus the most accurate reporter (smallest |d|, then id).
