A mean that cannot exist

Twenty-eight raiders answer one 1-to-5 question. Their sum is a whole number, so their mean must be a multiple of 1/28. A reported 3.47 falls between two possible values, and anyone with the table can see it.

GRIM test after Brown and Heathers (2017). All arithmetic is done in whole numbers, as in the lecture's code: the shown value of k/T is rounded to D decimals by integer division, so no floating-point error can flip a result. Candidate sums k run from floor(M × T) − 1 to floor(M × T) + 2. The survey, win rate and drop rate are invented.
