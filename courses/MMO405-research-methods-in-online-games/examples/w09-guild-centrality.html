Invented guild and hours, from the lecture's section 2 table. Ties are counted as in Ducheneaut, Yee, Nickell and Moore (CHI 2007): grouped in the same non-city zone, weighted by time together. Betweenness after Freeman (1977, 1978), normalised by (n − 1)(n − 2)/2 = 36 with n = 10 always. Eigenvector: start everyone at 1, then 200 rounds of x(v) + sum of neighbours' x, rescaled so the top member is 1. Degree, betweenness and eigenvector use unweighted kept ties; strength sums their hours.
