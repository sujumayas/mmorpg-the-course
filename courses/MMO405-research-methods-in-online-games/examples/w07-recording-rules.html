Same evening, two recording rules, two answers

You watch a guild for two hours and want the share of time spent arguing over loot. A scan sample lands on the true share on average. A one-zero score marks a whole interval if the behavior happens at any point in it, so it overstates the share by about 1 + T/b.

Abstract timeline, no real footage. Recording rules after Altmann (1974), "Observational Study of Behavior: Sampling Methods". Scan sampling is unbiased for time share; one-zero sampling scores an interval 1 when any part of a bout falls in it, so each bout of length b is counted for about T + b minutes. Shuffle uses the mulberry32 seeded generator: the same seed, L, n and total always give the same evening.
