# Invented manuscript extract for lab 11

"Raid Nights and Belonging in a Fantasy MMORPG Guild" (invented; no real study, guild or game data).

**Method (extract).** We surveyed all 28 active members of one raiding guild on a North American server during one content patch. Six held officer rank. Items used a 1-to-5 agreement scale. Raid enjoyment and loot fairness were single items. Guild belonging was the mean of four items. Voice chat use was the number of days in the last week the member joined the guild voice channel (0 to 7). Two members skipped the loot fairness item.

**Results (extract).** Table 1 gives the means (see `manuscript_means.csv`). Officers and members did not differ in raid enjoyment (T1) but officers rated loot fairness higher (T2). Belonging was above the scale midpoint (T3). Members who used voice chat on more days enjoyed raids more (T4, the correlation tested as t with 26 df).
