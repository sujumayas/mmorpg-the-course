# Manuscript for review (invented)

**Title:** Voice Chat Makes Guilds Last: Evidence from a Fantasy MMORPG

**Abstract.** Guilds are central to MMORPG play, but little is known about what keeps them together. We surveyed players about their guild's voice chat use and whether their guild still existed three months later. Guilds that used voice chat were far more likely to survive (p < .05). We conclude that voice chat causes guild longevity and recommend that developers integrate voice chat into their games.

## 1. Introduction

Guilds matter for retention (several studies). Voice chat is increasingly common in online games. However, no study has examined voice chat and guild survival. We hypothesize that voice chat increases guild survival because it builds social bonds.

## 2. Method

**Participants.** We posted a survey link on the game's largest subreddit and two Discord servers in March. 214 people responded. We removed incomplete responses, leaving the final sample.

**Measures.** Voice chat use: "How often does your guild use voice chat?" (1 = never, 5 = every session). Guild belonging: a five-item scale adapted from prior work (1 to 5). Guild survival: three months later, we checked each respondent's guild on the game's public guild directory and recorded whether it still existed.

**Analysis.** We split guilds into "voice" (voice chat use 4 or 5) and "no voice" (1 to 3) and compared survival with a chi-square test. We also correlated voice chat use with belonging.

## 3. Results

Table 1. Descriptive statistics.

| Group | n | Voice chat use M | Belonging M | Survived at 3 months |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Voice | 97 | 4.52 | 3.94 | 81% |
| No voice | 68 | 2.11 | 3.31 | 62% |

Voice guilds survived more often, χ²(1) = 7.10, p < .05. Voice chat use correlated with belonging, r(163) = .29, p = .21.

Figure 1 (described): two bars, "Voice" and "No voice", showing mean belonging with error bars. The bars do not overlap.

## 4. Discussion

Voice chat causes guilds to survive by building belonging. Developers should add built-in voice chat. A limitation is that the sample was from one game.

## 5. Ethics

Participants were adults who consented to the survey.
