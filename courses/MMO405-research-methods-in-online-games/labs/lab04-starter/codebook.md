# Guild Commitment Scale (invented for MMO405 Lab 04)

Response scale for every item: 1 = strongly disagree, 7 = strongly agree.

| Item | Wording | Scoring |
|---|---|---|
| g1 | I feel like I matter to my guild. | normal |
| g2 | My guildmates would notice if I stopped logging in. | normal |
| g3 | I enjoy spending time with people in my guild. | normal |
| g4 | I often feel like an outsider in guild chat. | normal |
| g5 | I would recommend my guild to a friend. | normal |
| g6 | I log in on raid nights even when I don't feel like playing. | normal |
| g7 | I would feel guilty if I missed a guild event. | normal |
| g8 | My guild expects me to keep my gear up to date. | normal |
| g9 | I play only when I want to, not because my guild needs me. | reverse |
| g10 | Leaving my guild would let people down. | normal |

The authors of the scale describe it as measuring one construct, "guild commitment".
