The fix that raised the error count

After the first fix, errors per tester jump from 1.48 to 1.74. At the same moment the number of problems left in the build drops. Testers now get past the fishing blocker and walk into problems that were there all along.

How sure is a fix?

Model from the UX201 week 8 lecture. Reach of a problem = product of (1 − p) over unfixed blockers before it; p_eff = reach × p. The Harbor tutorial and its problems are invented. The error spike after the first fix mirrors the RITE study of the Age of Empires II tutorial (Medlock, Wixon, Terrano, Romero and Fulton, 2002).
