Model from the UX201 week 7 lecture: recall = e^(−Δ/s), with s = s₀ × g^k after k practice uses. s₀ = 20 and g = 2.5 are teaching choices, not measurements. All-ready recall multiplies the four recall values and assumes they are forgotten independently. Only uses after the teaching minute count; uses at or after T do not count for recall at T. The abilities and the Warden fight are invented.
