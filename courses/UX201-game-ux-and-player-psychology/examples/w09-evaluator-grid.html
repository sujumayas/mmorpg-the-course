UX201 Game UX and Player Psychology · Week 9 · Worked example

Five evaluators, one raid screen

Each evaluator of the invented Tidefall raid interface found about a third of the problems. Together they found most of them. The overlap between two groups of evaluators estimates how many nobody found.

Presets
Sort columns

Group buttons cycle A, B, and excluded (–). Put one evaluator in A and one in B to test a single pair.

Simulation
Model: Nielsen and Landauer (1993), discovery across evaluators. Estimators: Lincoln-Petersen and Chapman capture-recapture; Chao (1984) lower-bound estimator. Tidefall and its 14 problems are invented for the lecture. In simulation, each problem's findability is drawn from a Beta distribution with the chosen mean and concentration κ = 2 / spread (so the variance is spread ÷ (2 + spread) of its largest possible value).
