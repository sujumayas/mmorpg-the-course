Every peak looks the same

A skin-conductance sensor flags 60 arousal peaks in a 25-minute horror session. They all look alike. Only 26.7% of them were fear. More came from frustration (33.3%). The sensor tells you when. Context tells you what.

Everything here is invented for teaching: the session, the coding and the trace. The trace is a slow random walk (3 to 5 µS) plus one skin-conductance response per peak window, starting 1.5 s into the window, rising over 1.5 s to 0.6 to 1.2 µS and decaying with a 4 s time constant. P(s | peak) = nshs / Σ njhj. Context counts are the fixed coded counts from the lecture (scare: 4, 12, 1, 3; death: 4, 2, 14, 0). See UX201 week 13, section 2.
