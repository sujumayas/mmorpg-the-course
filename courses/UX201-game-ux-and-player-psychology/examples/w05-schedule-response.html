Same average reward, different reasons to stop

Both players earn one reward per ten actions on average. The fixed player hits a low point after every reward and walks away. The random player's next action is always worth the same, so that low point never comes.

Model from the UX201 week 5 lecture: hyperbolic discount V = A / (1 + kD) after Mazur, averaged over the geometric wait for the random schedule. Pause and stopping rules are teaching simplifications. Both players are assumed to have chosen to start the session. A player's decision point comes right after each reward, which is where the fixed player's value is lowest.
