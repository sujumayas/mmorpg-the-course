How much should last month count?

Score = (r + a) / (r + s + a + b), where good and bad counts r and s shrink by λ before every match. Same history, two memories. The grey dashed line never forgets. The blue one keeps about 1/(1 − λ) matches. Each one gets a different player wrong.
