Bigger pools, fewer second meetings

A matchmaker puts you in a group of five for 20 runs. Grow the pool and you meet more people, up to all 80 partner slots. The same move wipes out repeat encounters: acquaintances fall from 8.87 at a pool of 60 to 0.07 at 1,000.

Model from the week 3 lecture: each run a player is grouped with m others drawn at random from N − 1, so p = m / (N − 1) and meetings with one person follow a binomial(r, p). Shadow of the future after Axelrod, The Evolution of Cooperation (1984), with standard payoffs 5, 3, 1, 0.
