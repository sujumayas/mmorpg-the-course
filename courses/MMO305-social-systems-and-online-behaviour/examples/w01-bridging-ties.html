MMO305 Social Systems and Online Behaviour · Week 1 · Worked example

Three ties connect a whole server

Four guilds of six. Add three cross-guild ties and the tie mix moves by under 5%, while the share of players who can reach each other jumps from 21.7% to 100%. Then log out the one player holding it together.

Designs from the lecture table

Presets keep their pattern when s changes, for example "last member of A to first of B". The lecture table uses s = 6.

Model: four complete-graph guilds with I = 4 × s(s − 1)/2. E-I index after Krackhardt and Stern (1988). Reach and average steps by breadth-first search from every player. A broker is a player whose removal splits the group they are in (an articulation point), so reach among the players who remain drops. Bridging and bonding after Granovetter (1973) and Putnam (2000).
