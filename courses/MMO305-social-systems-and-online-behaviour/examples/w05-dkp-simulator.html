Zero-sum DKP is earned-spent DKP with ρ = 1

ρ = n × e / (k × P) is points created per night over points spent. Above 1, veterans’ balances drift up, a newcomer starts far below them, and the red staircase goes flat. The wait grows with the guild’s age.

Model and rules from the MMO305 week 5 lecture: each present raider earns (e, or k × P / present under zero-sum), then the k present raiders with the highest balances each pay P for one item, ties to roster order. Every item is treated as an upgrade for everyone. DKP history: Warcraft Wiki, “Dragon kill points”; Malone (2009); Castronova & Fairfield (2006). Mixed attendance uses a seeded random generator, so a given seed always gives the same season.
