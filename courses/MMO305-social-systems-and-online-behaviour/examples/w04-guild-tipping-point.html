A guild has two stable sizes

Same recruiting, same churn rates, same leaders. A walkout of 12 in week 4 leaves the guild just under its critical mass of 20, and from there it slides to 10 instead of growing to 40. The net-flow plot shows why: the flow jumps from positive to negative at M.

Raid size presets (set M only)

Model from the week 4 lecture: N(t+1) = max(0, N(t) + R − c(N) × N), with c(N) = c_low when N ≥ M and c_high below. The walkout removes members at the start of its week. The drive adds its extra recruits to the step from week w to week w + 1, so with a walkout at week 0 the extra members arrive by week 1. Presets name raid sizes in text only: World of Warcraft's Mythic difficulty is fixed at 20 players since patch 6.0.2 (2014), Normal and Heroic flex from 10 to 30, and classic-era raids such as Molten Core took 40.
