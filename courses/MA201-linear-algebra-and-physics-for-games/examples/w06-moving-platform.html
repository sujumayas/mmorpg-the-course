Jumping off a moving platform

The same jump in two frames. In the world the arc slants. Seen from the platform it goes straight up and lands where it left, as long as the platform's velocity is added to the jump and the platform is not accelerating.

Model: exact kinematics with g = 980 px/s² (Godot's default 2D gravity) and a jump of 400 px/s relative to the platform (Godot's CharacterBody2D template). Leave modes follow Godot's platform_on_leave options. The caps follow LiftXCap and LiftYCap in Celeste's published Player.cs. Landing time is found by stepping 0.001 s on the exact formulas, then refining the crossing by bisection.
