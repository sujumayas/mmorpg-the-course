One FOV number, three meanings

Change the aspect ratio from 16:9 to 21:9. With a vertical FOV the wedge opens and the target pillar keeps its height. With a horizontal FOV the wedge stays put and the target grows.

What the FOV number means
Presets
Conversions from the week 4 lecture. Vertical: θ_v = N. Horizontal: θ_v = 2·atan(tan(N/2) / a). Horizontal at 4:3: θ_v = 2·atan(tan(N/2) / (4/3)). Then f = 1/tan(θ_v/2) and θ_h = 2·atan(a·tan(θ_v/2)). Target height on a 1080-pixel screen is 1.8 / 20 · f · 540. Engine defaults are cited in the lecture's sources.
