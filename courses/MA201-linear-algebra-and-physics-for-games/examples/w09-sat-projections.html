One gap on one axis says no

The boxes look like they touch, and their axis-aligned boxes overlap. The diagonal strip shows a gap. Two convex shapes collide only when every candidate axis shows overlapping shadows.

Presets

Model: box with center c, half-extents (hw, hh), angle θ; u = (cos θ, sin θ), v = (−sin θ, cos θ); r(n) = hw·|u·n| + hh·|v·n|; overlap on n = rA(n) + rB(n) − |d·n| with d = cB − cA. The table tests all four axes; an early-exit implementation stops at the row marked "separating axis". Method after Gottschalk, Lin and Manocha, "OBBTree" (SIGGRAPH 1996) and Ericson, Real-Time Collision Detection (2005).
