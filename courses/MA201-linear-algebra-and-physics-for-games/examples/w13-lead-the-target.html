Lead the target: solve |D + u·t| = s·t

The shot can reach anywhere on a circle that grows at speed s. The intercept is the first moment that circle touches the moving target. Aim at the body and you miss by the target's sideways motion. Aim at the one-step guess and you nearly hit. The exact root always hits while the shot is faster than the target.

Shots fly in straight lines at constant speed, no gravity. Hit radius counts centre-to-centre distance. The one-step and body-aim misses are the closest approach between shot and target centre over all t ≥ 0 after release. The dodge budget assumes a 0.25 s reaction time.
