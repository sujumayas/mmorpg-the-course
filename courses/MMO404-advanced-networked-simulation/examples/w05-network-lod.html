Invented constants. The hub (counts, distances, a = 2 m/s², sudden changes c per second, moving share) is invented but typical, as in the lecture. The walker's speed (3.5 m/s) and its cycle (1 s straight, 1 s turn, 0.6 s stopped) are invented for this page. The crowd summary's 12 cells × 8 B are the lecture's illustration.

Model from MMO404 week 5, section 2. Moving error-driven rate = min(20, max(0.2, √(a / (2θd)) + c)). Tiers: near 20 Hz × 20 B, mid 5 Hz × 12 B, far 1 Hz × 6 B. The walker view samples error at each 20 Hz server tick, after that tick's send decision; tiers extrapolate too. Heartbeat of 5 s after DIS (Aronson, 1997).
