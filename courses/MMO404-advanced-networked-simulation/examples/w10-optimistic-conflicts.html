The chest on the seam: forward or guess?

Two servers each grant claims on one chest at once, then tell each other. A claim conflicts when the other server granted one within the visibility window W. Most of W is waiting for the tick, so flushing claims immediately removes most conflicts without changing the players.

Red dots: two players were both told they won.
