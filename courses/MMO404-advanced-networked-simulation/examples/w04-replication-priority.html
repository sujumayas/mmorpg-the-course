Model from the week 4 lecture: p = w × f(d), f(d) = d0 / max(d, d0) with d0 = 10 m, rates by water-filling so that ρ = min(1, λp) and Σρ = B. Tick 50 ms (20 Hz). The packet animation runs at a quarter of real speed and picks each tick's entities with a priority accumulator: every entity gains its rate ρ each tick and the B highest are sent. Scope and priority by distance, size, speed and type after Frohnmayer and Gift, "The TRIBES Engine Networking Model" (Dynamix). Class weights, speeds and budget are teaching values.
