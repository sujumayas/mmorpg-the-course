What a headline says about the cost of one pair

Players, tick rate and game speed fix a ceiling on the CPU time per interacting pair. Put that ceiling next to a memory fetch and you see at once whether the headline needs an explanation.

    Invented or approximate. The week 1 zone uses that week's invented constants (true c_i = 2 µs). Custom is yours. The rungs are orders of magnitude from Norvig's table for a typical PC, not measurements of any game server. The bound assumes one core and that the pair term alone fills the budget.

    Case numbers as cited in the lecture: EVE Online, battle of B-R5RB (January 2014, 2,670 pilots, 10% time dilation) and FWST-8 (6 October 2020, peak 6,557 pilots in one system, as reported by CCP and Guinness World Records); EVE server tick 1 Hz; Second Life full region, 100 avatars and 45 simulator frames per second; Norvig, "Teach Yourself Programming in Ten Years", approximate timings. Formula: c_i ≤ 10⁹ × β / (f_t × d × n (n − 1) × g) ns.
