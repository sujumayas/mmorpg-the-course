Tank

Taunt sets the tank's threat to the highest threat on the table. The slopes stay the same.

Rules from classic-era World of Warcraft (1.12): 1 threat per point of damage times the threat modifier, target switch above 110% of the current target's threat in melee range and 130% at range, Taunt sets threat equal to the highest on the table. Sources: Warcraft Wiki, "Threat"; Warcraft Tavern, "WoW Classic Threat Guide & Reference Table". Constant rates are a simplification; real threat arrives in hits.
