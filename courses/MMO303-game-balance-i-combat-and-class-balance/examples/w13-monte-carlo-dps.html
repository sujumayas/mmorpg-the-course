Each hit: draw u from U(0,1), crit if u < p, deal D × m on a crit and D otherwise. Fight DPS = total / T. Theory: μ = nD(1 + p(m − 1)) / T and σ = D(m − 1)√(np(1 − p)) / T. Random numbers come from a seeded mulberry32 generator.
