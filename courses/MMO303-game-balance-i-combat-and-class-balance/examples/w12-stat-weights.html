Model: DPS = P × (1 + (m − 1)·c) × (1 + h), with c = 5% + crit rating / (100 × rating per 1%) and h = haste rating / (100 × rating per 1%). Weights are ∂DPS/∂stat ÷ ∂DPS/∂P. The class and its numbers are fictional teaching values, not data from a shipped game. Peak found by checking every whole rating point.
