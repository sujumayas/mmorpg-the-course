Your character uses the model of labs/lab09-starter/netsim.py without the snare: 60 Hz ticks, 6 m/s (0.1 m per tick), snapshots every third tick (20 Hz), inputs sent every tick, each direction half the round trip. B holds right for 700 ms, then left for 700 ms, repeating. Interpolation draws B at t − RTT/2 − D and extrapolates for at most 250 ms before holding, as Source does with cl_extrapolate_amount 0.25 (Valve Developer Community, "Source Multiplayer Networking").
