Model: week 1 lecture, section 3, and labs/lab01-starter/hol_sim.py. Fixed one-way delay, every packet acknowledged at once, the resend always arrives. Fast retransmit fires on the third duplicate ACK (packet k + 3). Linux-like RTO follows the Linux 200 ms floor on the variance term; RFC 6298 recommends a 1 s minimum. Real stacks add delayed ACKs, SACK and RACK, which move the constants but keep the stall.
