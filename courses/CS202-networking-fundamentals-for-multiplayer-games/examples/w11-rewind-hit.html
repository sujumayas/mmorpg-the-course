You hit what you saw. Did the server?

Left, your screen: the target where interpolation drew it, RTT/2 + D in the past. Right, the server when your shot arrives, another RTT/2 later. Rewind moves the target back by R = RTT + D before testing the ray.

Click or tap the shooter's screen to fire at that point. Space fires at the target's center.

Model: the server ticks at 60 Hz, records the target each tick and interpolates linearly between records. The shot's ray starts at (0, 0) and passes through the aim point. Hit if along = c · d̂ ≥ 0 and |c|² − along² ≤ 0.3². Rewind tests the target at t_arrival − min(RTT + D, R_max). W = arrival + RTTv/2 − (thidden − RTTv/2). The cover shot fires when 6 mm of the victim still shows past the wall edge (1 ms before fully hidden at 6 m/s), aimed 5 mm left of the edge. Rules after Bernier (2001) and the Source SDK 2013 lag compensation code.
