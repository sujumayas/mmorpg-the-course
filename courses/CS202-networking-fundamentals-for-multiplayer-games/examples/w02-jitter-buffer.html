Model: week 2 lecture, sections 2 and 3, and labs/lab02-starter/analyze.py. Percentiles are inclusive with linear interpolation, h = (n − 1)p. SD uses n − 1. RFC 3550 jitter: J += (|ei − ei−1| − J) / 16, in send order. Late means ei > B. Random data: e = U(0, 5), plus U(20, 80) with probability 0.06, from a seeded generator.
