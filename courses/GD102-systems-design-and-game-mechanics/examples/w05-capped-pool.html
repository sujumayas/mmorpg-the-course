Model: 180 s match in steps of 0.01 s. Each step the source adds rate × 0.01 (rate doubles from 120 s when the box is ticked). Anything above C is lost. The automatic player waits until the pool has been at C for H seconds, then spends c, all-or-nothing. Mana, energy and elixir systems in shipped card games share this structure; the numbers here are the fictional practice game from the lecture.
