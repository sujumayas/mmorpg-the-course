Model from the lecture: n = min(floor(G / c), W), G ← G + (p − c) × n, W = floor(270 / (2 − 0.1 L)) + 8 × sprinklers, one 28-day season, cycles that would end after day 28 are not run. The model ignores tilling, refilling the can and crop quality, so it is an upper bound on a real farm.
