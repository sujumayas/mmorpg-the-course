Rules modelled on the Catan base game (Catan GmbH rulebook, 2015 edition): a city costs 3 ore and 2 grain, the bank trades 4 identical cards for 1, harbors trade 3:1 or 2:1 for the named resource, and a roll of 7 makes anyone holding more than 7 cards discard half, rounded down. The hex layout is invented for the lecture. The ore player may also trade spare grain, lumber and brick at the bank 4:1. Rolls use a mulberry32 generator. Diagram notation after Adams & Dormans, Game Mechanics: Advanced Game Design (2012).
