Hand check: the two-racer recurrence

Gap next second = gap now + skill difference d − catch-up k × gap now. The gap settles at d / k.

Model: 8 karts, 120 one-second steps, 10 m/s base speed, skill from +0.35 to −0.35 m/s, noise with standard deviation 1.5 m/s. Distance rule: bonus = 0.1 × strength × gap to leader, gap capped at 60 m. Position rule: bonus = 2.0 × strength × rank / 7. Bonuses use positions before each move. Distance mode resembles the rule Mario Kart 8 Deluxe uses for item odds (Super Mario Wiki, "Spiny Shell (Mario Kart)"). All karts here are plain coloured lines. Seeded random numbers (mulberry32, Box-Muller), so batch 1 always gives the same table.
