    How often does it fire?

    "18 of 20" counts orders, not time. Two requests sent together start after independent random delays in [0, J]. They race when their starts differ by less than the window w: P = 1 − (1 − w/J)².

    —w / J
    —P per attempt
    —expected attempts 1/P
    —P(at least one in n)

    Semantics match DB201 Lab 06 (dupe_lab.py): the giver owns sword 5001, trade 1 gives it to Bram, trade 2 to Cyra. A turn for a finished trade is skipped. Under the row lock, a blocked turn goes back in the queue and the other trade moves first, as in the lab's scheduler. The sword is a generic shape.
