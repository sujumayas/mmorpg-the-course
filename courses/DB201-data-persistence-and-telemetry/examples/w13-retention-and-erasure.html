Keep data longer, pay on every erasure

Storage grows in proportion to the retention window. Triple the days and you triple the terabytes. Erasure by full scan grows with it and runs into the 720 hours a month has. A pseudonym table keeps erasure flat at every retention window. Pick retention and schema together.

Model from the lecture. A scans every event row per request (or once a week for a batch). B looks up the player's rows through a 24-byte-per-row index on player id. C deletes two rows from a pseudonym table, which leaves the event rows unlinkable. The 720-hour ceiling is 30 days of round-the-clock scanning; the dotted line at 30 days marks the one-month response deadline.
