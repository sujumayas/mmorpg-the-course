One trade, two save timers

A gives B a sword. Each character is saved on its own timer, so a crash can restore one side with the trade and the other without it. Half an interval after the trade, that split is a coin flip.

—save interval T
—crash delay d
—d / T
—p = min(d/T, 1)
—P(dupe)
—P(loss)
—P(split)
—outcome, these phases

Arrow keys move a slider by 1% of T. A character's first save after the trade happens at its phase.

Cases from the lecture

Model from DB201 week 5, section 2: each character saves every T seconds at an independent phase, and a crash restores the last save of each. With one transaction per trade, both sides share one commit point. The sword is a generic shape.
