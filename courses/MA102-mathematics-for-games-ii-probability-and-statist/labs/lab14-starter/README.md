# Lab 14 starter

- `tutorial_test.csv`: 4,800 new players (`player_id`, `group`, `completed_tutorial`, `day1_return`, `sessions_day1`, `platform`), randomly assigned 50/50.
- `ab_template.py`: functions to fill in for steps 2 to 4 and the power calculation. If you prefer a spreadsheet, build cells for `k_A`, `n_A`, `k_B`, `n_B`, pooled rate, SE, `z`, p-value, and a second sheet for the power formula.
- `mulberry32.py`: the seeded generator the course interactives use. Use seed 14.
- `make_tutorial_test.py`: instructor only. Regenerates the data; edit its constants to make a fresh cohort.
