Model: P(proc on attack N) = min(1, C × N), with N reset to 1 after a proc, as described for Dota 2 on the Dota 2 Wiki (Random distribution page). C is solved here by bisection so the mean gap equals 1/p, not copied from a table. Random numbers come from a seeded mulberry32 generator, so a seed always reproduces the same strip.
