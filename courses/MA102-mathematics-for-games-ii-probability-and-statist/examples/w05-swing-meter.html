Same damage per hit. Not the same kill time.

Both weapons average 20 damage per hit. The swingy one crits, so it often lands its last hit with damage to spare. Against a health pool, that overkill changes the average number of hits to kill.

Mean fixed at 20. Swingy base b = 20 / (1 + c(m − 1)) = 16

Crit hit = m × b = 32

Steady needs ceil(H / 20) = 5 hits

Model: each hit deals b, or m × b on a crit with chance c, independent of other hits. The enemy dies on the first hit where total damage reaches H. P(T ≤ n) sums binomial probabilities over crit counts k with b(n + k(m − 1)) ≥ H. Weapons are abstract; no game data is used here.
