Am I due for the drop?

Long dry streaks are common when every attempt is rolled fresh, yet after any streak the next attempt is still exactly p. A bag with the same long-run rate makes the player truly due, and it caps the drought.

Model: independent rolls succeed with probability p every attempt. A bag holds N = round(1/p) tickets with one winner, drawn without replacement and refilled when empty. The streak bonus is a teaching model, chance = min(1, p + b × k), in the spirit of the miss-streak help in XCOM 2: War of the Chosen on easier difficulties (see the lecture). All simulation uses a seeded generator (mulberry32), so a seed always reproduces the same players.
