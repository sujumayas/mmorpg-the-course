Kills until the drop

The most likely kill to get a 1% drop on is the first one. Yet at the "expected" 100 kills, a third of players still have nothing, and the tail runs past 300.

100kills (cursor n)
—have the drop by n
—still dry after n
—dry players (turn on population)

The numbers

StatisticValueHow it is computed

Logarithmic scale from 1/5,000 to 1/2.

Successes needed

Above 1, the wait follows the negative binomial distribution. Percentiles come from adding up the bars.

Model: each kill is an independent trial with the same chance p.
