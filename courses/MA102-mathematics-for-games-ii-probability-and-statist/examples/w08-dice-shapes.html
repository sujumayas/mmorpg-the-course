Same bars, different curves

2d6 and 1d12 cover almost the same range, and both succeed 41.7% of the time on 8 or more. The bottom chart shows the difference. One die gives a straight line, two dice give an S-curve, so a +1 bonus is worth twice as much in the middle of 2d6.
