Is advantage worth +5?

Roll two d20, keep the higher. Each square on the left is one of the 400 equally likely pairs. Against a target of 18, advantage succeeds 27.75% of the time and a flat +5 succeeds 40%. The two only agree at T = 11, apart from T = 1, where every roll succeeds.
