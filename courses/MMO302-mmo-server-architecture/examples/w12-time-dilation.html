Model: W = a·n + b·n², f = clamp(C / W, f_min, 1), backlog growth = max(0, f·W − C), command delay = backlog / C + 1 / f. Controller, one step per real second: f ← f + α(clamp(aim·C / W, f_min, 1) − f), then backlog ← max(0, backlog + f·W − C), with 500 pilots rising to n at t = 10 s.

The cost constants a and b are invented for teaching. The real-game facts are from CCP Veritas, "Introducing Time Dilation (TiDi)" (EVE Online dev blog, April 2011); CCP Logibro, "Battle for 6VDT-H: summary and numbers" (July 2013, 4,070 pilots, one-tenth speed); Wikipedia, "Battle of B-R5RB" (January 2014, peak 2,670 in system); CCP Gun Show, "Tranquility Tech III" (October 2015, Everest nodes); EVE University Wiki, "Time dilation" (10% floor). Nothing on this page measures CCP's servers.
