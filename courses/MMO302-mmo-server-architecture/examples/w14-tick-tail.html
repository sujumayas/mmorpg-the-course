Percentiles use the nearest rank: sort ascending and take item ⌈q × n⌉. A stall is a tick longer than the budget B = 1000 / f. The seconds-with-a-stall formula assumes stalls are independent; with clustered spikes the page also counts the real share of 1-second windows (blocks of f ticks) that contain one. Random ticks come from a seeded generator (mulberry32), so a seed always gives the same strip. Lecture data: 970 ticks of 12 + (7i mod 13) ms, 20 of 45 ms, 10 of 140 ms.
