How much does fog of war leak?

Reveal later and honest players get shot by enemies they cannot see. Reveal earlier and cheaters see them first.

One enemy, 100 s round: visible 3 s in total, within 50 m for 40 s, in "could be visible" PVS cells for 10 s.

Model from the lecture: T = 1000 / tick rate, τmin = ping / 2 + T, pop-in = max(0, τmin − τ), line-of-sight leak = peeks × τ. It ignores interpolation delay and assumes an exact visibility test. The look-ahead design follows Riot Games' April 2020 post on VALORANT's fog of war; the corridor, enemy path and round numbers are invented for teaching. The corridor view is schematic, and its loop timings are not to scale with the round table.
