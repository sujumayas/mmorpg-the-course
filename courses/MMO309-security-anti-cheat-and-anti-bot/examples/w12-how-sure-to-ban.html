How sure do you need to be to ban?

A detector that is right about 99% of honest players leaves you less than 50% sure of any one player. Evidence multiplies the odds. Each sanction sets its own bar. The same evidence can justify a 24-hour trade lock and still fall short of a ban.

Log scale, 0.01% to 20%. Scanning a flagged queue has a higher base rate than scanning everyone.
