Show triage

Accounts at or above t are acted on. Accounts from t_review up to t go to human review. The band's lower edge is set by what reviewers can clear.

Model from the lecture, section 2: precision = Rπ / (Rπ + F(1 − π)), with R = 1 − Φ(t − d) and F = 1 − Φ(t) for human scores N(0, 1) and bot scores N(d, 1). Φ uses the Abramowitz and Stegun 7.1.26 erf approximation. Dot positions come from mulberry32 with seed 8; flagged dots are the first ones in that seeded order, so the counts are round(R × bots) and round(F × humans).
