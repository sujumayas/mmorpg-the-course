Rollback, trace or ban

A rollback's cost climbs in a straight line with every hour of delay and lands on the whole server. A trace's cost shoots up, then levels off, lands on a few buyers and always leaves the vendored share behind. They cross at one delay. Neither one touches what the ring keeps. Only a ban sized to its proceeds does.

Trace inflation = 100 (1 − b) m D p / M. Ring net = keeps − A − debit, where debit = min(wallet, keeps). Crossover is the first T on a 0.01 h grid where the rollback's harm exceeds the trace's. Numbers are a teaching model, not data from any game.
