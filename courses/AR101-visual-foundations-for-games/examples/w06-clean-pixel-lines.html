Why pixel art loves 26.57°

A 30° line on a pixel grid can never be clean: its runs come out 1, 2, 2, 2, 1, 2, 2, 2 and the eye sees it bend. The 2:1 line gives up 3.43° and gets runs of 2, forever. Tap “2:1” to see it.
