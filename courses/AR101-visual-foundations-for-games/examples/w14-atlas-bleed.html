Gutters: cheap per sprite, expensive at the power of two

With no gutter, the stone tiles pick up a green line from their atlas neighbor as soon as the texture shrinks one mip level. One pixel of gutter fixes level 0 and pushes 64 sprites from a 256 atlas to a 512 one, four times the memory.

On: the stone starts on a multiple of 16, the case the model's derivation assumes. Off: it starts where the packed grid puts it (one cell + g), with no rounding, and the first bleeding level then depends on luck. With these sizes it comes at the model's level or later (g = 1 stays clean at level 1), so treat the aligned case as the guarantee.
