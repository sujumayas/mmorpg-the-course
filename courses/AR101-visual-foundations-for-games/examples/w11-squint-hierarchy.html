Hue names a tier. Weight ranks it.

The eye ranks labels by weight, area times value contrast, not by what their colors mean. Blur the scene (a squint test) and only the heavy labels survive.

Presets
Model: AR101 week 11. W = plate area × |L*plate − L*ground| + s² × |L*text − L*behind|, where behind is the plate if shown, else the ground. Step rule: each tier ≥ 1.5 × the one below. Hue is cosmetic here: it tints text, plate and border at the L* you set. Tier colors in the WoW preset are the item-quality hex codes documented by the Warcraft Wiki ("Quality"). Item names are invented.
