Danger reads from points

A triangle has one number that says "not round" (C = 0.605) and one that says "pointed" (θ = 60°). Press 3 : 1 bar. Its C drops below the triangle's, yet the creature looks like a sturdy block. The corners decide the reading, not roundness.

compactness C
sharpest corner θ
area A
perimeter P

The last step is a circle.

Presets

C = 4πA/P² with A from the shoelace formula and P summed over segments. Rounded corners and arcs are drawn with 64 segments per quarter turn, so C can differ from the exact value in the third decimal. θ is the smallest interior angle among convex corners that are still corners. Rounded ones and arc points do not count. Reading rules from the lecture: θ ≤ 65 sharp, 75 to 105 square, C ≥ 0.85 with θ ≥ 120 round. The creatures are invented stand-ins.
