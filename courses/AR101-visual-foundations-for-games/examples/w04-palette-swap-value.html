Swap the hue, keep the value

The right slime is the left one with every hue turned 120° and S and V left alone. It came out darker, and its darkest step now sits 8.8 L* above the black outline, so it melts into it. Switch to keep value and the two grayscale strips become the same.

HSV to RGB as in the lecture's section 2, each channel rounded with floor(x × 255 + 0.5). L* as in week 1: sRGB linearization, Y = 0.2126 R + 0.7152 G + 0.0722 B, L* = 116 Y^(1/3) − 16. Keep value bisects V (40 steps) for the smallest V that reaches the original L*. If V = 100 is still too dark it bisects S instead. The slime is an invented figure.
