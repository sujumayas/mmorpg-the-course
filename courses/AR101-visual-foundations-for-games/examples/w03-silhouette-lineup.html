Pixel editor

Click a pixel in the lineup to add or remove it. With the keyboard, Tab to a character, move with the arrow keys and press Space. Edits work at size 1.

Masks, normalization and IoU follow the lecture's section 2. Each mask is aligned at its lowest row, centered on that row's middle column. Half size merges 2 × 2 blocks and keeps a block when at least 2 of its 4 pixels are filled. The four characters and their colors are invented for this page.
