      Game record

      The magic-square mapping and the counts (8 triples; 4, 3 and 2 triples per number) come from exhaustive enumeration, as in the lecture. The game is known as Three-to-Fifteen, Pick 15 or Number Scrabble. Herbert Simon used it in The Sciences of the Artificial (1969). The Perfect computer uses minimax. Among equally good moves it takes the number in the most triples, then the lowest number.
