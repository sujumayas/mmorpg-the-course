The telegraph budget

A windup gives the player a fixed number of milliseconds. Reaction, choosing between warnings, the dodge's start-up and display lag all spend it. So do effects that hide the warning. Add attack types or add a hit flash, and the same bar crosses the same line.

Model: time needed = hidden + a + b × log2(n) + start-up + lag. The b × log2(n) term is the Hick-Hyman law (Hick 1952, Hyman 1953). Values of a, b and lag are teaching defaults from the lecture, not measurements of any game. Frame counts round the needed time up, so a verdict of "fair" is never optimistic by a partial frame.
