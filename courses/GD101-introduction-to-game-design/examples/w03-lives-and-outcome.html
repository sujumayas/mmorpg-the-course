Lives slide the win curve along the skill axis

Each curve is one starting-lives setting. Add lives and the curve keeps its S shape and moves left. Lives decide which players get a close finish, not how hard the game is.

The table W(n, L), built cell by cell

Rows: stages left n. Columns: lives L. No 1-up. Each cell is p × the cell above + (1 − p) × the cell to its left. Hover or focus a cell (arrow keys move); click or press Enter to set L. The red ring is your current setting.

Check the model by playing

Each square is one run with the current settings. Green means the run finished, red means it ran out of lives.

Press the button to play 200 runs with these settings and compare the count with the model.

Win chance vs skill

Skill p is the chance of clearing one stage. The shaded band is 20% to 80%, where the outcome is uncertain.

—win chance W(n, L)
—with 1-up
—uncertain band (20% to 80%)
—skill p at 50% win

Model from the lecture: W(0, L) = 1, W(n, 0) = 0, W(n, L) = p·W(n − 1, L) + (1 − p)·W(n, L − 1). Every stage has the same p and players do not improve during a run. Crossings are found by bisection on p. Dice presets match the in-class activity: two dice, clear a stage by rolling at least the target.
