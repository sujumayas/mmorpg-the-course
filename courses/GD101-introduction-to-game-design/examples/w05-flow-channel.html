Skill: S(n) = 10 − (10 − S0) × gn. Smaller g learns faster. Everyone plateaus at 10.

C = S × (0.9 + 0.4 × ((n mod 5) / 4)). R cycles 0.9, 1.0, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3 every five rounds.
