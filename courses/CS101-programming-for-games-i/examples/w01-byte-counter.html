Every box has an edge

An 8-bit level counter sits at 254. Two presses of +1 and level 256 is stored as 0. On the right, a character at 16,777,216.3 lands on 16,777,216, because a 32-bit float that large has no fractions left. Integers wrap at a hard edge. Floats blur gradually.
