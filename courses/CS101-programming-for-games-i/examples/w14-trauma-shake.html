Bug on: T -= d / 60 each frame, whatever the frame rate.

Model after Squirrel Eiserloh's trauma approach (GDC 2016) as used in the lecture: T = min(T + amount, 1), T = max(T − d·dt, 0), offset = max_offset × scale × Tp × noise(t × 15). Noise is 1D value noise with smoothstep, seeds 1 and 2 for x and y. The simulation steps at the chosen rate with a fixed dt, whatever your display does. Only the camera canvas moves. Keys: G graze, B big hit, C custom.
