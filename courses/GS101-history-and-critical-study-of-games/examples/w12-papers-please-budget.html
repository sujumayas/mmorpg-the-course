Model: fine(k) = 0 for k ≤ 2, 5 for k = 3 or 4, 5(k − 3) from k = 5. Income = 5(n − c) − P(c), plus 20 in Easy mode. Care = rent 25 + heat 10 (Class-8 apartment) + 5 food per relative. Savings(d) = savings(d − 1) + surplus.

Steady-state footnote: the model charges rent 25 on every day (the game charges 20 until the end of day 5) and ignores medicine and illness. The line below 0 is the cost of full care you could not cover. The game ends only if rent itself cannot be paid. Heat and food are optional and get dropped first, in this order of protection: rent, son's food, heat, then wife, mother-in-law, uncle, niece.
