Model: P = R × e / f, P_world = max(0, P − C), A = P_world / ρ, d = √(2 × P_world / (ρ × θ_rad)). The visible ground is a circular sector of angle θ and radius d. d_ref is d at the same settings and 30 fps. Throughput presets are Sony's published PlayStation peak figures (360,000 flat-shaded and 180,000 texture-mapped polygons per second). Efficiency and density are assumptions, not measurements. The model ignores culling, level of detail and fill rate, so it explains a pressure, not any one game's draw distance.
