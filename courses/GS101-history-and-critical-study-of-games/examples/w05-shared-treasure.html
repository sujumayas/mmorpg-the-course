Shared treasure: when other players become competition

One treasure pool, refilled every reset, split among everyone online. Below the crossover N* other players cost you nothing. Above it, income falls as 1/N and the world sits empty.

Only the 102,400-point wizard threshold is MUD1's. The pool, rates and period are illustrative.

Model: N* = V / (f × L); income = min(f, V / (N × L)); pool empties at V / (N × f); dry share = max(0, 1 − empties_at / L); hours to wiz = 102,400 / income. The 102,400-point threshold comes from the British Legends help pages (the CompuServe-era MUD1, now revived). Everything else is invented for teaching.
