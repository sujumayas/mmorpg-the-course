Same subscribers, two true stories

On a linear axis World of Warcraft's line keeps climbing steeply into 2007. The bars beside it show the growth rate falling from the very first period. Flip the axis to log and the curve agrees with the bars.

Who counted as a subscriber

Blizzard's definition in this period, paraphrased:

  • Anyone who had paid a subscription fee or held an active prepaid card.
  • Anyone inside the free month that came with buying the game.
  • Internet Game Room players who had accessed the game in the last 30 days, counted the same way in licensees' territories.

Excluded: free promotions, expired or cancelled accounts.

More than half of the January 2008 total (5.5 / 10 = 55%) was in Asia, where many players bought time by the hour in internet cafés. A café player who logged in once in 30 days counted the same as a monthly payer at home. Asia's share alone was 10 times EverQuest's all-time peak.
