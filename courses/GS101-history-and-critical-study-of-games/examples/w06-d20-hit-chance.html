GS101 History and Critical Study of Games · Week 6 · Worked example

One d20, twenty faces, 5% a step

Hit if d20 + B ≥ AC. A natural 1 always misses and a natural 20 always hits. Each +1 to hit adds one face of the die. What that face is worth depends on how many faces you already had.

Every +1 adds 5 points of hit chance. At B = 0 it adds 16.7% damage; at B = +10, 6.25%.
