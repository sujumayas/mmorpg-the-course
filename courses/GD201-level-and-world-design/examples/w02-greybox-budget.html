When to stop greyboxing

Grey rounds are cheap, so they clear layout problems fast. Some problems only show up once art is in, and those need expensive art rounds. With 80 hours, the fewest problems at ship come from switching to art at 16 h. Leave too early or too late and the count rises on both sides of the U.
