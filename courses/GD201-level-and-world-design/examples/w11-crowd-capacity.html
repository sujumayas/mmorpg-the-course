Crowding happens at the camps

A hundred players in one square kilometre sounds empty, and the open ground is. The eight camps are not: each player there sees about 32 times as many neighbours. Drag the population up and the camp line leaves the comfortable band long before the open ground feels busy. Make the map bigger and capacity does not move.
