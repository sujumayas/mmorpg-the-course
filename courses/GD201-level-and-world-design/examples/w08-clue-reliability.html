Four clues can reach 96% of players or 5%

Same four clues, same watchtower. The only thing that changes is how many of them a player must notice to get the story. You see every clue. Your players don't.

Clue tiers

Reading rule

When on, half the players are attentive and notice clue i with chance min(1, 1.6 p), the rest with 2p minus that. Each clue keeps its average p, but a player who spots one clue is likely to spot the others.

Model from the week 8 lecture: the chance that at least k of n clues are noticed, clue i with chance pi, by the p_at_least dynamic program. Tier chances (Gate 0.95, A 0.8, B 0.5, C 0.25, D 0.1) are the lecture's starting values for a teaching level, not measured data.
