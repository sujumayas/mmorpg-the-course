Model from the lecture. A hero hit deals base × mult with the crit chance, else base. Exact hits-to-kill comes from dynamic programming over damage dealt. The hero wins if hits to kill ≤ enemy swings when the hero goes first, ≤ enemy swings − 1 when the enemy goes first. Interval 1.96 × sqrt(p(1 − p) / N). Random numbers come from mulberry32, so a seed reproduces a run. Doom shotgun and Imp health from the Doom source release (linuxdoom-1.10, 1997), Doom and Doom II era; the real game draws from a fixed 256-entry random table, so its rolls are close to uniform, not exact.
