Legendary: 12× the danger, 4× the length.

The menu says Legendary makes enemies hit three times harder. It also cuts your damage to a quarter, and the two multiply. Your fights get twelve times as dangerous and four times as long.

TTK = TTK₀ · h / p. TTD = TTD₀ / e. R = TTD / TTK. Health cost = 1 / R. Ratio vs Adept = p / (h · e). Length vs Adept = h / p. Moving p slides a point diagonally. Moving e slides it straight up or down.
