Magma Rager. Priced 2.5 by the line. Played as much worse. The line cannot see that 1 health makes attack useless.

Three minions sit exactly on the line, so the designers priced attack and health at half a mana each. A linear price list prices each stat alone. It misses that a 1-health body dies before its 5 attack matters.
