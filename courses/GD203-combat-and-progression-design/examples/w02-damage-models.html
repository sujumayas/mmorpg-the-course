Subtractive armor retires weak enemies. Divisive armor never does.

Ten swarm hits of 20 and one ogre hit of 200 carry the same raw damage. At 20 armor, subtraction erases the swarm and barely dents the ogre. Division cuts both by the same share.

Formula
Divisive constant

f = K / (A + K). The same fraction for every hit size.
