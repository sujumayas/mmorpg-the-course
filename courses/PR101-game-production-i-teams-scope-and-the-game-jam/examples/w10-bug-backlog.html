The bug list keeps growing while everyone fixes at full speed

After feature lock, testers find fewer bugs each day. Until the daily finds drop under what the team can fix, the open list rises. It peaks on the day the bars cross under the fix-capacity line. Regressions, bugs caused by fixes, sink that line more than extra capacity lifts it.
