The same estimates draw a cone in one view and a pipe in the other

Every dot is one checkpoint of a finished jam task. On the left, actual total ÷ estimated total closes in on 1 because the hours already spent are known exactly. On the right, actual remaining ÷ estimated remaining for the same checkpoints stays as wide at the end of a task as at the start.

—Total view: band width p90 ÷ p10, first bin to last. It narrows.
—Remaining view: same checkpoints, same bins. It does not.

Hover, tap or focus a chart and use the arrow keys. The same checkpoint lights up in both views. Home and End jump to the first and last checkpoint.
