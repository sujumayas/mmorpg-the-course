When an hour of team work returns less than an hour, free riding pays

Each hour you put into the project returns m / n from the shared result plus k in individual credit. In a bigger team that falls under 1, and doing nothing beats contributing for each person, even though everyone prefers the team where all contribute. Individual credit lifts the curve, and the lift you need grows with team size.
