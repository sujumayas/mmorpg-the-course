    c is off while s = 0. The decision-map curve assumes s = 0.

    Tpatch = G × (φ × m + 1 − φ) / F. build = Tr / (1 − s). carried = c × s × F × build. Trebuild = build + max(0, (1 − φ) × G − carried) / F. m* = Tr × F / (φ × G). Scenarios A, B and C are the lecture's teaching cases, not measurements of Final Fantasy XIV or any other game.
