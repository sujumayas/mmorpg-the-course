    Ebeta = testers × hours; Elaunch = players × hours. A bug that fires once per N player-hours is a Poisson process, so P(seen in beta) = 1 − exp(−Ebeta / N) and expected launch firings = Elaunch / N. Rule of three: with zero events in E hours, the 95% upper bound on the rate is 3 / E (Hanley and Lippman-Hand, JAMA, 1983). Numbers are the lecture's teaching scenario, not data from any one game.
