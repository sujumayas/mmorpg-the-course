The rebuild slows where the recordings stop

A fan team rebuilding a closed MMO from scratch moves fast through every behaviour somebody recorded, then crawls through the ones it has to guess. Evidence gathered before the shutdown moves the finish line as much as a bigger team. The server source puts the project on a different line altogether.

 

—V, volunteer-hours per week
—H, hours needed
—T, weeks · years to finish
—t_q, faithful part done (years)
—T_s with source, weeks · years
—extra weeks per missing point of q

Presets

Share of behaviours somebody recorded or documented before the servers closed.

Model and default values from the MMO407 week 11 lecture, section 2. The game is invented. H = B × w × (q + g × (1 − q)); T = H / V weeks; t_q = B × q × w / V; T_s = B × w_s / V. A year is 52 weeks. The team rebuilds evidence-backed behaviours first, at V / w behaviours a week, then the guessed ones at V / (g × w).
