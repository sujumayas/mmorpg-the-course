AU101 Audio for Games · Week 5 · Worked example

Your gun fires on the frame grid

An 800 rpm gun wants a shot every 75 ms. At 60 fps the game loop only checks every 16.7 ms, so a cooldown that resets waits 5 frames and the gun fires at 720 rpm. Carrying the leftover time fixes the rate but makes the rhythm limp. Scheduling on the audio clock fixes both.

Strategy
No sound until you press Fire or Compare. The timeline shows every shot either way.
The strategy decides the times; playback always uses the Web Audio clock, so the page adds no jitter of its own. Times are computed with integer arithmetic (units of 1 / (fps × rpm) s, or whole mix chunks) so ceil() never trips on float rounding. The click is a 25 ms, 2 kHz sine burst with a 5 ms decay plus a little noise, synthesised in the page.
