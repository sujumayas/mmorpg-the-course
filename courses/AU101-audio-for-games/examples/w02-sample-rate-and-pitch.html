Pitch down, and the top falls in

A 48 kHz recording holds nothing above 24 kHz. Play it one octave down and that edge lands at 12 kHz, well inside what you hear. The sample rate you record at decides how bright a pitched sound can stay.

Sound is off until you press a play button.

The jingle is 40 synthesised sine partials from a fixed seed, filtered at f_top and quantised to N bits with no dither.

Model: f_top = min(fs / 2, f_mic, f_source); after pitch p the top is p × f_top. Storage is fs × (N / 8) bytes per second per channel. Dynamic range 6.02 × N dB is the ideal figure for N-bit quantisation. The spectrum shape L(f) = −6 − 18 × f / 60 kHz dB is a drawing aid, not a measured key recording.
