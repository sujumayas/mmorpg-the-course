Crossfading engine loops by RPM

At 1,500 RPM a linear crossfade sets two loops to 0.5 each. The gains add to 1, so the curve editor looks balanced, but the mix is 3 dB quieter. Switch to equal power and the dip disappears. The second plot shows the other cost: with only three loops, each one gets stretched by up to 7 semitones.

Engine tones are synthesized in the browser: the firing frequency of a four-cylinder four-stroke (RPM / 60 × 2), harmonics 1 to 12 at 1/k, a 3 Hz wobble and band-passed noise. SuperTuxKart rule from the SuperTuxKart source, release 1.5 (src/karts/kart.cpp, Kart::updateEngineSFX): pitch = 0.6 + 0.35 × (0.9 f + 3 × (f mod 0.333334)), clamped to 0.5 to 2.0. No game audio or art is used.
