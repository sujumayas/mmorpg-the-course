Whose sounds get a voice?

A 40-player raid wants 68 voices and the budget is 32. Under a flat first-come policy your own sounds are dropped as often as a stranger's 30 m away. Switch to Tiered: you keep every sound, and the room still sounds just as busy.

Reservations (Tiered only)

Model from the week 12 lecture. Fixed 20 Hz tick. Each player acts every g seconds from a seeded random phase (seed 1) and each action starts a requests. Little's law: demand = players × a × W / g. The boss holds 4 requests at all times. Flat keeps the oldest requests up to the budget. Tiered first fills each tier's reservation nearest first, then spills remaining slots down in the order you, boss, party, others. Party size is fixed at 4. Stats cover the whole action cycles inside the last 10 s (9 s at g = 1.5 s), so measured demand matches Little's law when nothing bursts. They restart when you change a setting. All sound is generated with the Web Audio API; there are no audio files.
