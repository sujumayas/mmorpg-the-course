All four stems are synthesized in the browser at 120 BPM in 4/4, a 4-bar loop of A minor, F major, G major, E major. A lookahead scheduler (25 ms timer, 100 ms ahead) places every note on one shared clock, and gains move with setTargetAtTime (15 ms) every frame. The model: g(x) = clamp((x − a) / w, 0, 1), thresholds a = 0.2 (drums), 0.45 (bass), 0.7 (lead), pad always on. No recorded or game music is used.
