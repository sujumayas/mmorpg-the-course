A quantized switch waits. A stinger doesn't.

One press shows you one wait, which tells you nothing. Press "simulate 1,000 presses" and the histogram shows the rule. With a 4-bar phrase at 120 BPM the switch waits anywhere from 0 to 8 s, 4 s on average, and more than 1 s in 87.5% of presses. A stinger on the next beat answers within half a second while the real switch still waits.

Sound is off. The clock, timeline and histogram run anyway.

Model: U = k × 60 / BPM with k = 0 (immediate), 1 (beat), 4 (bar) or 16 (4-bar phrase); next_sync(t) = ceil(t / U − 10⁻⁹) × U, measured from music start; a stinger uses k = 1. A lookahead scheduler (25 ms timer, 100 ms ahead) switches loops at the sync point with a 10 ms fade on the old loop. Both loops and the stinger (A5, C6, A6) are synthesized in the browser. No recorded or game music is used.
