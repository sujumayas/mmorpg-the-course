The cone of confusion

A sound 30° to your front right and its mirror 150° behind you reach your ears with the same timing difference. Timing alone can’t tell front from back. Turn your head and the two split apart.

Sound is off until you press a play button.

Same delay, opposite sides of your head.

Cue model

Headphones recommended. Each ear gets its own channel, so speakers blur the cue.

Model: spherical head, ITD = (R / c)(θ + sin θ) with lateral angle θ = asin(sin a), R = 0.0875 m, c = 343 m/s (Woodworth's formula). The level difference is a simple 6 dB × sin a teaching model, not a measured head response. No pinna filtering is modelled, which is why front and back stay identical here.
