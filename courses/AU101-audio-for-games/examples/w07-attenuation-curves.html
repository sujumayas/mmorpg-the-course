Curve formulas follow the lecture: inverse r / (r + f(d − r)), linear with a hard stop at R, the tapered minimum of both, and a Godot-style inverse capped at +3 dB with an optional linear taper to R. Pan is a plain equal-power stereo pan computed from the listener's right-hand vector, so what you hear is exactly the model on screen. The "hard edge at 32" preset mirrors the reach of a volume-2 sound in the Minecraft hook; the curve shape inside Minecraft's radius is not modeled. Footsteps are band-passed noise synthesized in the browser. No game audio or art is used.
