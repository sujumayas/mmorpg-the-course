Formulas from the week 14 lecture. PCM size P = count × sample rate × 2 bytes × channels × length (16-bit). Compression ratio r: PCM 1, ADPCM 3.5, Vorbis 10. Package = P / r. Resident: decompress on load P; compressed in memory P / r; stream 200,000 bytes per voice. Voices of a decompressed group play as PCM. Stream reads = voices × rate × 2 × channels / r. 1 MB = 1,000,000 bytes, 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. The test sound is noise plus a 6,000 to 200 Hz sweep built in code; lower rates average the 48 kHz samples in each output window before decimating. No audio files.
