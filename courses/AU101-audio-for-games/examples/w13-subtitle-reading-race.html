    Formulas from the week 13 lecture. n counts every character including spaces and punctuation. need = max(d_min, n/R). hold = max(0, need − L). Gap g = next start − (start + L); the scene ends at 17.8 s. A line clashes when hold > g. Unread (voice-locked) = max(0, 1 − R·L/n); under Hold the display time is min(need, L + g); under Hold + stack it is need. The missed list starts at the beginning of the word the reader was in, since half a word is not read; the Unread column counts characters. The voice is procedural: one filtered sawtooth blip per vowel group. No recorded audio.
