MA101 Mathematics for Games I · Week 8 · Worked example

Remap is one shared fraction

Landing speed becomes squash through one number, t. The input marker, the t marker and both scale markers always sit the same fraction of the way along their own shaded ranges, so the connector runs straight down. Push past 240 and watch it bend.

Arrow keys change v by 1, Page Up and Page Down by 20.

Sources: NoelFB/Celeste repository, Player.cs (published 2018): landing squash min(speed / 240, 1), scales lerped to 1.6 and 0.4, recovery Approach(scale, 1, 1.75 × dt), wall slide lerp(160, 20, timer / 1.2). Team Fortress 2 wiki, damage page: modifier 1.5 at 0 HU, 1.0 at 512 HU, 0.5 at 1,024 HU and beyond. Khronos, OpenGL 2.1 glFog reference: GL_LINEAR fog factor. All shapes here are generic.
