An orbit is cos and sin of a growing angle

Each orb sits at (r cos θ, r sin θ). Its height traces the wave on the right. A bigger ring opens wider gaps between orbs, yet any spot on the ring still gets hit the same number of times per second.

Degrees bug on. The angle is passed to cos and sin as degrees, with no conversion to radians.
