MA101 Mathematics for Games I · Week 3 · Worked example

A curved jump, steered by a straight line

You pick apex height h and time to apex th. Gravity and launch speed follow: g = 2h/th², v₀ = 2h/th. Drag time to apex down. The arc keeps its height and the speed line below gets much steeper.

Model built from published constants; the game has more rules. Gravity 900 px/s², jump speed 105 px/s held for 0.2 s, half gravity while speed is under 40 px/s and jump is held, fall speed capped at 160 px/s. The designer sliders are off in this mode; click the button again to return.
