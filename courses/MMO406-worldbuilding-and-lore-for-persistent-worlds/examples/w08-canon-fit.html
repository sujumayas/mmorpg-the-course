Player types

Loyalists pick the canon option with probability qL on every choice, rebels with qR. The per-choice sliders are off in this mode.

Model: canonized set C; F = product of pi over C; E = sum of (1 − pi); dissonance bits = sum of −log2(pi). The distribution comes from the recurrence d'[k] = d[k] × pi + d[k − 1] × (1 − pi), which assumes choices are independent. The mixture mode drops that assumption: each type is binomial, and the population is their weighted sum. Choices are generic; no game is modelled.
