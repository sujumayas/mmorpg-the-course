Shared syllables are not shared sound

The style bible says the Vessari and the Durhk share a third of the Vessari syllables. Players hear names, and names are built from the syllables a culture uses most. Count the weight, not the syllables.

Direction A → B: how often an A name could pass as a B name.
—ocount, shared by count
—o, shared by weight
—Pamb(L), could pass as B
—I(L), identifiable

Names

With a marker, a share m of names ends on a syllable only culture A uses. Those names can never pass as B.

Pamb(L) = (1 − m) × oL
