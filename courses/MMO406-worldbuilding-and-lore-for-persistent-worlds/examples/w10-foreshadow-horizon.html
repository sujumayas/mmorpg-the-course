Hook type
Presets

Presets set ρ and the hook type. They keep your lead time and v.

Model from the week 10 lecture. s = 1 − ρ. E(d) = s^d × v × d − (1 − s^d) × D. The continuous optimum is d* = −1 / ln(s) − D / v, floored at 0. Best whole d is the lowest d from 0 to 10 with the largest E. All rates and values are invented for teaching.
