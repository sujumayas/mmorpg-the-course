One myth sounds sure. The truth needs two.

Twenty facts about the founding of the invented city of Ossary, told in the temple accounts of its four river cults. Each account states some facts truly, bends some, and stays silent on the rest. A reader holding one account cannot tell which is which. A community comparing accounts can, but only where two accounts agree.
