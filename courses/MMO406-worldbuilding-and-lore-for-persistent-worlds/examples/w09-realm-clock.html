Model from the week 9 lecture. Daily rate R = N × q × u. Without fatigue a realm finishes on day ceil(G / R). With fatigue, participation falls by a factor d each day, so a realm can never deliver more than R / (1 − d) in total. Realm sizes and rates are invented for teaching.
