All populations are invented. The model assumes players search independently; in practice, a discovered loophole spreads by word of mouth, which makes use after discovery faster still.

Formulas: H = N × h, E = qH, P(found) = 1 − e−E, wait at peak = 1/(Cq), H50 = ln 2 / q, P(group) = 1 − (1 − f)n. Law names after Raph Koster, "The Laws of Online World Design" (raphkoster.com).
