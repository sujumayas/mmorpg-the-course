In a square of 50 players, 90% chance someone is your twin.

Share of players who keep the default option in each category. The rest spread evenly over the other options.

—M, looks promised
—c, chance two players match
—Meff = 1/c
—E, expected twin pairs
—P(at least one twin)
—crowd size for even odds (n50)
—kept every default
—pairs in the crowd

Invented creator. Categories are assumed independent; real players' choices are correlated, which usually lowers Meff further.

Model: cj = Σp² within a category (the chance two random players picked the same option), c = product over categories, E = n(n−1)/2 × c, P(twin) = 1 − e−E, n50 = ½ + √(¼ + 2 ln 2 / c). With a default kept by share d, cj = d² + (1−d)²/(mj−1). All categories and numbers are invented for teaching.
