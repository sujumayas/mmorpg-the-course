MMO301 Virtual Worlds · Week 1 · Worked example

2,000 players online, and a leveling player sees nobody 80% of the time.

A world feels shared when someone is near you now. That depends on λ, the expected number of other players in your view, not on how many people are logged in. Pool or split realms and watch the map fill or empty without adding a single player.

Parameters are illustrative. Real players cluster, so real maps are lumpier than this one.
