MMO301 Virtual Worlds · Week 4 · Worked example

Four guilds share one weekly boss: 53.3 weeks per item, against 13.3 in an instance.

In contested content the wait for loot grows in a straight line with the number of guilds. Instanced content stays flat. The two are identical at one guild. Drag the strongest guild's share up and a race makes the wait worse for everyone except the winner.
