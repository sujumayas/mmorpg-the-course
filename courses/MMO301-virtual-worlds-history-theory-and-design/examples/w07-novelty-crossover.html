Authored content runs out when you play more. Player-made content runs out when players leave.

The flat line is new authored content from the studio’s patches. The rising curve is new situations other players create. Push play time up and the crossover slides left. Press “Launch year to year three” and watch your dot slide down the sandbox curve while the themepark line does not move.

Log-like steps: 1 player below 300, then 2, 5, 10, 20, 50.
