A death that costs only time can slow you down. It can never send you back.

The solid line is a time-only penalty, like World of Warcraft after patch 1.1.0. The dashed line adds lost experience, like classic EverQuest. Drag the cursor on the left chart past the dashed line’s break-even rate and watch the session on the right end below where it started.
