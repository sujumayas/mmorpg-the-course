MMO301 Virtual Worlds · Week 8 · Signature worked example

The killing rule decides the world, not the starting mix.

A 1,000-player world starts with more killers than socialisers. Under open killing the socialisers are nearly gone within a month. Then the killers run out of prey, and the world settles at less than half its size. Drag protection to 90% and the same players build a social world instead.
