MMO301 Virtual Worlds · Week 3 · Signature worked example

The bank ran dry on day 37. Players hold 99.5% of everything, and the world can only spawn what they give back.

In a closed loop, daily supply settles at d × R: the share players give back each day, times everything that exists. Designers set R. Players set d, and hoarding pushes it toward zero. The faucet-and-drain fix keeps supply up and moves the problem into an ever-growing hoard.

Mechanism from Simpson (1999) on Ultima Online's first-year economy. All numbers are illustrative.
