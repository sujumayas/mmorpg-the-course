MMO301 Virtual Worlds · Week 2 · Worked example

200 builders never run out of places to go, and fill the disk in 24 weeks.

Letting players build wins the race against content running out once the population passes N*. Then it starts losing a second race, against storage. Restricting who builds barely moves N* and widens the window between the two enormously.
