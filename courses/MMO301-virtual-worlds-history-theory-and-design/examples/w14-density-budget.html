All values are invented for teaching. No real game's numbers are used. Model: P = P_peak × share(hour), field = (1 − h) × P, E = field × πr² / A, copies n = ceil(E / E_max) when dynamic copies are on, E per copy = E / n. Share is interpolated linearly between the listed hours and wraps at 24. Dots are placed uniformly at random from a fixed seed (13).
