The ring in the centre of money mode is a plain shape standing for any money good. In Diablo II: Lord of Destruction, players settled on the Stone of Jordan, a unique ring with fixed stats that every class wanted, so trades were quoted in it even though the game had gold. The model does not care which good wins. It only needs everyone to accept the same one.

Model from the week 9 lecture: barter succeeds per meeting with chance 1/(G−1)², so it takes (G−1)² meetings on average; money splits a trade into a sale and a purchase, each with chance 1/(G−1), for 2(G−1) meetings counting both halves. Simulation uses a seeded random number generator (mulberry32), so the same seed gives the same histogram. Stone of Jordan facts: Blizzard Entertainment, The Arreat Summit (classic.battle.net), Diablo II: Lord of Destruction, patches 1.10 to 1.14.
