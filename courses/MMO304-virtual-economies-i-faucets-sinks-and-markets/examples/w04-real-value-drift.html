The inflation rate calms down. Your gold keeps shrinking.

Faucets add a fixed amount of gold per player each day, so the daily price rise gets smaller in percent. Look right, though. Every fixed gold number (a newcomer's income, a mount price) keeps losing real value. Only a stock sink stops the slide.
