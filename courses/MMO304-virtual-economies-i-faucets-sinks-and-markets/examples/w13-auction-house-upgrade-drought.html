The auction house that emptied the loot hunt

A market lifts your gear to the top almost at once. It also divides the rate at which your own drops turn into upgrades by 1 + m. The left panel shows what players see. The right panel shows what they lose.

each of your drops is matched by m on sale
Model from the lecture, section 2. Drops have a uniform quality percentile. With K items in reach, you wear quality K/(K+1) on average and the next own drop upgrades with chance 1/(K+1). Cumulative self-found upgrades after n drops are the harmonic number Hn. The market player's are Hn/(1+m), counting the first purchase as already made. Numbers are illustrative, not Diablo III drop tables.
