Repairs slow the drift. Taxes stop it.

A repair bill scales with hours played, so it only trims the faucet. A trade tax scales with the money people hold, so it puts a ceiling on gold per player. While new players keep arriving with empty pockets, both look like they work. The dotted line shows the same rules once new players stop.
