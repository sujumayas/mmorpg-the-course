Model: item power is uniform on (0, 1), so power equals percentile q. Both players see the same drops (seeded mulberry32). Before each of the market player's drops, the pool gains M − 1 other drops, drawn as one value U1/(M−1), the maximum of M − 1 uniforms. A drop is self-found if it beats everything in the pool; otherwise the player buys the pool's best item whenever it beats their own. Items and players are generic.
