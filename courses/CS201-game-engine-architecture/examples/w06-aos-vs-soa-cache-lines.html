Array of structs vs struct of arrays

A loop over one million entities pays for whole cache lines. With the lecture's 128-byte entity, AoS fetches 128 bytes to use 24. SoA fetches 24 and uses all of them. Predicted speed-up: 5.33× on 128-byte lines, 2.67× on 64-byte lines.

Model from the week 6 lecture, section 2: AoS with s ≥ L fetches L × ceil(h / L) bytes per entity, AoS with s < L fetches s, SoA fetches h. It assumes each struct starts on a line boundary and the data is too big to stay cached. It leaves out write-back of modified lines and SIMD, which is why the measured 1,000,000 row shows 15× where the model says 5.33×. Measured times are from bench_layouts.cpp in lab 06's starter, Apple clang 17, September 2026.
