Model from lecture section 1: by hand, each of F fields is written into each of S systems, F × S sites; reflected, one declaration per field and one loop per system, F + S. Each hand edit is missed independently with chance q, so a field has a hole with chance 1 − (1 − q)^S. The 2% miss rate is the lecture's assumption, not a measured value. "Fields with a hole" counts rows with at least one hole, not hollow squares. Holes come from a seeded hash, so they stay put while you change F, S and q.
