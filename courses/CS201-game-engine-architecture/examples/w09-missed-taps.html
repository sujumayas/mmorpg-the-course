CS201 Game Engine Architecture · Week 9 · Worked example

Polling once a frame misses short taps

A tap that starts and ends between two frame samples never shows up as "held". An event queue records the key-down itself, so it keeps every tap and its true time.

Numbers

Taps are placed by a seeded random generator (mulberry32, seed 9, plus one per re-roll) with uniform, non-overlapping start times. The observed miss rate and mean delay come from a hidden 200-second run with the same settings. Formulas: miss rate max(0, 1 − d/T), mean delay T/2, aim error ω × T / 2 on average and ω × T at worst.
