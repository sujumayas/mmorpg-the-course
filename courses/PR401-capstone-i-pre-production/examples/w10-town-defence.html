Can a town defend itself at 8 p.m.?

A town of 15 expects 3.77 members online in the evening, just under the 4 a raid needs. That small gap makes defence a coin flip. Try each lever: a bigger town, more play in the window, a militia that lowers the number needed.

p = e × h / W in the evening, (1 − e) × h / (168 − W) off-peak. The militia lowers m by its size, never below 1.
