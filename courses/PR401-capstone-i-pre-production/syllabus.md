# PR401 — Capstone I: Pre-production

Year 4, semester 7 · 4 credits · Prerequisites: PR301

Capstone teams pitch, prototype and plan an MMO-scope project.

## Learning outcomes

- Produce a greenlit pitch, technical plan and prototype
