Advanced: hour weights
Model from PR301 week 9, section 4. Sessions start uniformly within each hour, weighted by time of day, and last an exponentially distributed time with mean W hours. R(T) counts humans online at clock hour T. At the event hour, a share e of all players who ever log in are online, plus the regular crowd among the rest: C(E) = (1 − e) × R(E) + e × P. With no event, sign-ups are sized at the last event hour chosen with e = 0. The hand model in the guide (Little's law) gives slightly different sign-up counts because it assumes the evening crowd has reached its steady level. Saltmarsh is an invented team.
