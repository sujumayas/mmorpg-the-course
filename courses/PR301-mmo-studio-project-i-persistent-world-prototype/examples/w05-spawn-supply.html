Will the camp feed the crowd?

Saltmarsh's Reedbank camp has 12 wolves on a 90-second timer. It looked perfect with three testers. Push the crowd up: a fixed timer leaves players standing idle once demand passes supply, while a dynamic timer holds utilization at 0.8 until it hits its floor.
