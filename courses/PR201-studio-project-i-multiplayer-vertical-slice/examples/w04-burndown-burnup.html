Formulas from PR201 week 4, section 2. Burn-down forecast at day k: R[k] − ((R[0] − R[k]) / k) × (D − k). Burn-up forecast: S[k] − done[k] − (done[k] / k) × (D − k). The Hollowmarch sprint is invented for teaching. Generated model: done[d] = r1 × min(d, m) + r2 × max(0, d − m), capped at scope; scope is S0, then S0 + x from day a.
