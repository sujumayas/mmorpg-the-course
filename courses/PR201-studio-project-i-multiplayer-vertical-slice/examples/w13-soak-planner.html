Model from PR201 week 13, section 3: λ_upper = factor(k) / T, P(no crash) = e^(−λ_upper × S), λ_max = −ln(R) / S, T_needed = factor(k) / λ_max. Factors are the 95% Poisson upper bounds for k = 0 to 3 (2.996, 4.744, 6.296, 7.754), computed by bisection as in the guide's script and lab 13's soak_target.py. The grid uses a seeded mulberry32 generator (seed 13 at start). The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook of Statistical Methods, section 8.3.1.1, prints the same factors rounded to 3.00, 4.74, 6.30 and 7.75.
