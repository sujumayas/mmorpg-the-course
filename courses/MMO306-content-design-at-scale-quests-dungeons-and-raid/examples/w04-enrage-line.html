What is the enrage timer testing?

Group A brings 9% more damage than group B. On Quakemaw it still hits the enrage wall, because every mechanic flattens its line for longer. A fight with mechanics is a tank-and-spank against a boss with hidden health H / u, and that hidden health is different for every group.

u = 1 − x × tax  ·  Tkill = H / (P × u)  ·  Simulation: 0.1 s ticks, one mechanic every 20 s from 10 s, each group idle for x × tax × 20 s per cycle (at most 19.9 s). A death removes 1/14 of paper DPS from 60 s on.

Stitchgut, Quakemaw and both groups are invented for teaching, as in the lecture. The pattern they stand for comes from classic World of Warcraft's Naxxramas (patch 1.12): Patchwerk, a pure damage check with a 7-minute enrage, and Heigan the Unclean, a movement fight with no listed enrage (Warcraft Wiki).
