The best farm keeps climbing

Model from the lecture: each mission pays r = 1 + X with X exponential, mean m. The median is 1 + m ln 2 and the expected best of N is 1 + m HN, where HN = 1 + 1/2 + … + 1/N is summed exactly. Above 5,000 missions the histogram draws 5,000 and scales the bars; the best is then one exact draw of the maximum, m × (−ln(1 − U1/N)). The table applies the cap and pool when they are on.
