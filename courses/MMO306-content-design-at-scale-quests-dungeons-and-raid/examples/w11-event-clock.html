The event clock

Space a world boss closer and more players can reach it, but each fight is thinner. Past the knee, spacing it wider only shuts people out.

Model from the lecture: a player online for S minutes catches an occurrence if one starts between W minutes before they log in and the moment they log off, so reach = min(1, (S + W)/T) and crowd = N × min(S + W, T)/1440. Sessions in the strip use a fixed seed; the 60 samples approximate reach, the readouts are exact. Planners are extra players who log in for the planned occurrence (the one nearest 20:00).
