How the board is drawn

Model: p = a / S. Expected exposure after d days X(d) = 1 − (1 − p)d for a fresh random draw each day, min(1, d × a / S) for a fixed cycle. Day of 90% exposure = ln 0.1 / ln(1 − p), or ceil(0.9 × S / a) for a cycle. The “not offered yesterday” mode has no simple formula, so its expected curve is the average of 400 seeded runs and its 90% mark is the first whole day that reaches 90%. Pool sizes and daily counts are the lecture's figures for each system. The board uses numbered cards and plain shapes, not game art.
