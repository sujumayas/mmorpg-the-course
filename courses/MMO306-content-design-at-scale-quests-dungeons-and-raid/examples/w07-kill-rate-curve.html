Where boss health tuning bites

Drag health. Watch how much the kill rate moves.

—boss health H
—required DPS R = H / T
—z = ln(R / m) / σ
—kill rate K
—points per 1% health
—sample groups that can kill

Upload bias: groups above the median upload 80% of their logs, groups below upload 20%. The chart then shows what a log site sees.

Model: required DPS R = H / T, divided by (1 + e) with a damage buff. z = ln(R / m) / σ, kill rate K = 1 − Φ(z), Φ from the Abramowitz and Stegun 7.1.26 erf approximation. The slope label is 100 × φ(z) × 0.01 / σ: a 1% health change moves ln H by about 0.01, which is how the lecture's table is computed. The twenty sample groups are the invented guilds from the lecture, not real data.
