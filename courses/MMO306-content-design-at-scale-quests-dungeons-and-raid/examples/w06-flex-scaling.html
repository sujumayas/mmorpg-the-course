Flex scaling: health and mechanics pull in opposite directions

Scale boss health with raid size and small groups need more damage per dealer. Keep every mechanic per player and big groups miss more often. One formula fixes the first problem. Nothing fixes the second for free.

Health rule

Health scaling can be made neutral with one formula. Mechanic scaling has to be decided one mechanic at a time.
