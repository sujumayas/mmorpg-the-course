Roll back early, claw back late

A dupe put 20% extra money into the economy. Every hour you wait, a rollback erases more honest play, so its cost climbs in a straight line. A clawback's leftover harm climbs fast, then levels off, because the money can only leak once. The lines cross, and the crossing tells you which response to pick.
