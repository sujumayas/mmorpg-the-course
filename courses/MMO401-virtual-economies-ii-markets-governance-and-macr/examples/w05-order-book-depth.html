B. Break-even spread

A market maker buys at bid B, sells at ask A, pays broker fee b on both orders and sales tax t on the sale. Profit is zero when A / B = (1 + b) / (1 − b − t).

—break-even A / B
—break-even spread

Scale 0% to 20%. The herb book's spread as a ratio is 10.0 / 9.8 − 1 = 2.04%, about 2.0%. As a share of the 9.9 mid it is 2.02%.

The herb book and linear depth values are invented for teaching.
