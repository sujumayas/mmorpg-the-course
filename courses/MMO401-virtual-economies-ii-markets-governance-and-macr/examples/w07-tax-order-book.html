The book says the tax is cheap. The sink curve disagrees.

A 20% tax still leaves 44 of 50 trades crossing on the order book, so it looks harmless. Let players trade around the market and the sink peaks at a much lower rate, then falls. How hard bypass is sets the rate you can charge.

Presets (set the rate only)

Bypass cost is spread evenly from 0 to E across trades. A trade leaves the market when the tax on it, t × Pb, is more than its cost of trading directly.

Model: linear demand P = 100 − Q and supply P = Q, tax on the buyer's price, bypass share b = min(1, t × Pb / E). The order book is the discrete version: 100 buyers and 100 sellers with one unit each. Preset rates as cited in the lecture: Old School RuneScape Grand Exchange 2% (from 29 May 2025), World of Warcraft auction house 5% cut (current retail), EVE Online base broker's fee 3% plus 7.5% sales tax (from 12 March 2025), Guild Wars 2 trading post 5% listing plus 10% exchange fee. The model uses one combined rate on the sale price.
