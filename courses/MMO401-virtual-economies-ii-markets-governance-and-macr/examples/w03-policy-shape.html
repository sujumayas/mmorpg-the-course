Hand check (lecture section 2)

Four players with 100, 200, 300, 400 gold. f = 25, r = 10%, no trading.

Rules. Each trade picks a player i and a different player j uniformly and moves d gold from i to j if i holds at least d. After each tick: A sets x to x − r·x + f. B sets x to x − r·x + (f·N)·x/M, with M the total before the policy. C sets x to max(0, x + r·x − f). All policies share one seed, so they see the same trade sequence (common random numbers). Model and reference values from MMO401 week 3 and lab 03.
