Raise a sales tax. Where does the money settle?

A proportional tax moves the money stock to a new level, then collects exactly the faucet again. Slide the elasticity from 0 to 1 and watch the price and volume lines swap places while nominal trade lands on the same number.

0: players ignore the tax1

ε = 1 means trade falls as fast as the tax rises.
