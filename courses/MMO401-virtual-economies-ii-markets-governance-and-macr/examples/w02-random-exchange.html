Model: Dragulescu and Yakovenko, "Statistical mechanics of money," European Physical Journal B 17 (2000). Each trade picks payer i and a different payee j uniformly; if gold[i] ≥ d, d gold moves from i to j. Predictions assume an exponential distribution with mean T = m0: share below mean 1 − 1/e, median T ln 2, top-10% share 0.1(1 + ln 10), Gini 0.5. The broke share uses the discrete-chunk result 1/(T/d + 1). The browser generator differs from Python's, so exact values differ from the lecture table while matching it within noise.
