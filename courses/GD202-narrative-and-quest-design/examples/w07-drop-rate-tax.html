Model from the week 7 lecture: kills K to collect n items at drop chance p follow a negative binomial distribution. P(K = k) = C(k − 1, n − 1) pn (1 − p)k − n. Percentile q is the smallest k with P(K ≤ k) ≥ q. Quest designs are invented placeholders.
