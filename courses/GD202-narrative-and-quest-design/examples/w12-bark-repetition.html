The rule picks the repeats, not the line count

A guard has five idle barks and says one 40 times. Pick each bark by dice and the player hears the same line twice in a row about 8 times. Deal them from a shuffle bag and repeats can only happen at the seams between bags. To promise that the first five plays are all different nine times in ten, dice need 97 lines.
