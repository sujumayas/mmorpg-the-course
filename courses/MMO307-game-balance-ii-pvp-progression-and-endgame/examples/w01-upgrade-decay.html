Model from the lecture: one run gives one item for a uniformly random slot (random), the best of m such items (choice), c tokens toward items that cost q (tokens), or a drop with chance d plus c tokens, buying as soon as the player holds q (hybrid). Random and choice curves are exact, from the Markov chain over slots filled. Hybrid numbers come from 20,000 simulated players, seed 7. Game contexts named in the lecture: World of Warcraft's Great Vault (a weekly choice) and Final Fantasy XIV's tomestones (tokens, Dawntrail patch 7.05).
