Weekly cap or rolling cap

Missing six weeks costs 4,500 forever under a weekly cap, and nothing by week 12 under a rolling one.

—catch-up week w* = vj / (v − L)
—late player finishes (week)
—veteran finishes, N / L
—free lateness Jfree = N/L − N/v (weeks)

Model from MMO307 week 6. The allowance of 750 per week follows World of Warcraft Shadowlands Valor (patch 9.0.5, Warcraft Wiki); the finish line N and the late player are invented. Weeks are continuous: a player earning v per week has earned v/2 halfway through the week.
