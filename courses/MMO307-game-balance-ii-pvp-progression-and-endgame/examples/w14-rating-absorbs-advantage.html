The rating eats the win rate

A class that is 60 rating points stronger wins 58.55% of its games only while its players still sit at their old ratings. When every game on a ladder is on that class, the players climb until the class wins exactly 50%. The advantage has not gone. It has moved to the top of the ladder, and a deeper cutoff shows it more.
