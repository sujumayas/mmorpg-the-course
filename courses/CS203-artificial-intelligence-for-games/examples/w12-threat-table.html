The healer never hit the boss. The boss’s damage did this.

The healer heals what the boss deals, so healing threat grows with boss damage. Whether the boss ever turns to the healer depends on one ratio, S = 2.6 × m × d × N / B. Below 1 the healer always pulls in the end. Above 1, never.
