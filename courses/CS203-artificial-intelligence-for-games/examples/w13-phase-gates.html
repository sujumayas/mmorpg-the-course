The Warden and its numbers are invented for the lecture. Cast rule for HP gates: a cast at phase time t happens if t × D < Hi, all integers, so thresholds land exactly. Time gates: a cast at absolute time T happens if it is inside its phase window and T × D < H. Both fights end at the kill or the enrage, whichever comes first. Onyxia (HP-gated, 65% and 40%) and Ragnaros (time-gated, submerge at 3 minutes) in the lecture are the classic World of Warcraft examples; nothing here draws them.
