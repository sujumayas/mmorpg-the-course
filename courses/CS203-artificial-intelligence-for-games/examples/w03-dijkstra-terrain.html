Algorithms as in the week 3 lecture code: neighbours in N, E, S, W order, pay the terrain cost to enter a tile, mountains are not neighbours. Dijkstra uses a binary min-heap ordered by (cost, column, row) with lazy deletion and runs until the heap is empty. Terrain costs follow the hex-and-counter wargame convention the lecture uses; all tiles are flat colors and letters.
