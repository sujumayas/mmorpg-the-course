Timer routine after Don Hodges' disassembly of the Donkey Kong arcade ROM (Nintendo, 1981): level × 10 + 40 in an 8-bit register, capped at 80 hundreds only after the overflow, level counter capped at 99. His patch clamps the level to 4 before the arithmetic. Source: D. Hodges, "How High Can You Get? The Fix for Donkey Kong's Kill Screen" (2007, updated 2017). Bars are drawn generically; no game art.
