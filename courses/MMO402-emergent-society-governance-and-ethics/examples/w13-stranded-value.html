Notice is a refund paid in time

Stranded value falls with the square of the life that is left. The first month of notice relieves 23 times more than the last one, so the cheapest fair plan mixes some notice with a smaller cash refund.

Presets

Presets set only the values they name and keep the rest.

—V(0), no notice
—V(N)
—share left, V(N) / V(0)
—running cost C × N
—total(N) = C×N + k×V(N)
—best notice N*
—total(N*)
—stranded share of spend
All spend rates, costs and lifetimes are invented for teaching and describe no real game. The presets borrow only the shape of each case from the lecture (a game live two weeks, about 6.3 months of notice, a community server release). Model: V(N) = (1 − ρ) × s × (m(1 − N/L) − m²/2L), with m = min(T, max(0, L − N)); it assumes sales stop on the day of the announcement.
