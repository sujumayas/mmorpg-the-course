The gate and the default

Children exposed to stranger chat = N × π × (1 − se) × k. The age gate and the chat default multiply. Turning chat off by default costs nothing, and when children are rare even an accurate check restricts more older players than it catches children.

Gate

se = 1 − ℓ, sp = 1 (no adult claims to be a child)

Chat default for flagged-as-13+

Presets

k is the share of missed children who end up in chat with strangers. Fortnite-style = self-declared gate, chat off by default (the 2022 FTC order).

All rates are invented for teaching, as in the lecture: ℓ = 0.5, se = 0.90, sp = 0.95, k on = 0.9, k off = 0.3. Real age-estimation accuracy varies by vendor, age band and population. Fortnite reference: U.S. Federal Trade Commission press release, 19 December 2022 (chat off by default for children and teens). No game art is used.
