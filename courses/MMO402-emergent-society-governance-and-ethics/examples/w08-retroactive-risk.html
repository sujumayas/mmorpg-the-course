A 1% risk per technique is a 78% risk per career

Koster's article 8 says no punishing acts that were allowed when done. Here is why that matters. A small chance that any one new technique gets banned later adds up fast for the players who try the most techniques, and the policy decides who carries it.

Model from the lecture. Retroactive: any detected past technique later judged an exploit earns a ban, P = 1 − (1 − r)^N. Warn first: the first detection earns a warning, the second a ban, P = 1 − (1 − r)^N − N r (1 − r)^(N − 1). Notice first: techniques are banned only after public notice, so nothing done earlier is punished. Article numbering follows Raph Koster, "Declaring the Rights of Players" (2000).
