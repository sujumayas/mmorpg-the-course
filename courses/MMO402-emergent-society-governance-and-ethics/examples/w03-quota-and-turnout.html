A bloc's seats depend on everyone else's turnout

The bloc stays at 6,000 voters, 2% of the electorate. As casual players turn out, its share of ballots shrinks and its guaranteed STV seats step down: 5 at 2% turnout, 3 at 5%, none at 30%. Switch to block voting and the staircase becomes a cliff.
