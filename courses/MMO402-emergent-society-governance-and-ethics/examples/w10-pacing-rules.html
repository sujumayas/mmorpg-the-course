Rested, tired or on time?

Each pacing rule reads a different clock. Rested experience reads time offline, so every arrangement of 14 hours earns the same. Fatigue reads time in session. Daily rewards read the calendar, and a missed day cannot be made up.

Rested numbers: Warcraft Wiki, "Rest" (retail World of Warcraft): one bubble (10% of a level) per 16 hours in an inn or city, cap 150% of a level, doubled kill experience.
