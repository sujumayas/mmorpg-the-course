Human review works only while the queue keeps up

Forty moderators can review 11,200 reports a day. Send them more than that and the queue grows every day without limit. Send them less and the reports you skip are either punished by a machine or ignored. Pick a policy and watch where 100,000 daily reports go.

When overloaded, reviewers take reports in proportion from every review band. Missed counts only dismissed bands on day one; violations stuck in the backlog are shown separately. The spare-capacity option assumes violations are spread evenly through the band, so it is a lower bound.

Band shares and violation rates are the lecture's teaching numbers for a generic game, not a real studio's data. Formulas: C = M × H × 60 / a; backlog(d) = max(0, d × (load − C)); wait = backlog / C.
