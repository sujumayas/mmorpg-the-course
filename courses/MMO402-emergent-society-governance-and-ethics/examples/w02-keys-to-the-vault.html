Keys to the vault: K of N officers

Require more officers to sign off and the chance of a hijack falls fast. The chance that enough officers show up in time holds, then collapses near K = N × a. The cheapest rule depends on what lateness costs you. Try "Upkeep duty" and watch the best K move.
